# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors arriving from social profile bios, recruiters, peers, and potential collaborators who need a fast path to Ranielli Montagna's main public links from mobile or desktop.

## Product Purpose

This site is a first-party personal links page for `links.ranimontagna.com`. It replaces a third-party Linktree profile with an owned, static, branded, high-performance page that points people to the portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and X / Twitter.

## Brand Personality

Technical, focused, and polished. The interface should feel familiar enough for social bio traffic, but more deliberate and owned than a generic link aggregator.

## Anti-references

Avoid Linktree platform chrome, generic template cards, noisy decorative animation, fake terminal overload, gradient text, glassmorphism as decoration, and motion that delays access to links.

## Design Principles

- First-party credibility: the page should clearly feel owned by the Ranielli Montagna brand.
- Fast path to action: links remain the main interface and should never be hidden behind motion.
- Technical restraint: animation and styling should communicate precision, not novelty for its own sake.
- Mobile-first confidence: the page must feel complete and stable in a social-bio mobile context.
- Accessible by default: reduced motion, contrast, keyboard focus, and readable hierarchy are non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep semantic links, preserve visible focus states, maintain strong contrast on the dark surface, and avoid horizontal overflow on small screens.
